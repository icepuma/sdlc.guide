## Summary
State the problem, recommendation, main trade-off, and decision requested.

## Context, goals & non-goals
Describe what is changing, why now, how success will be measured, and what is out of scope.

## Proposed design
Explain behavior, interfaces, data, failure modes, compatibility, and enough detail to review the approach.

## Alternatives & prior art
Include viable options, the status quo, relevant precedent, and why each is not preferred.

## Risks & mitigations
Cover relevant security, privacy, reliability, performance, cost, and operational impact.

## Rollout & operation
When relevant, state migration, testing, enablement, observability, ownership, and rollback.

## Decision outcome
